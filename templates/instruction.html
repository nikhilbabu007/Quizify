Quiz Instructions

Please read each instruction carefully before starting your quiz.

01

Total Questions

The quiz contains 15 multiple choice questions divided into three difficulty levels.

  • 5 Easy questions
  • 5 Medium questions
  • 5 Hard questions
02

Time Limit

You have a total of 5 minutes to complete the entire quiz.

  • The timer starts when the quiz begins.
  • Manage your time carefully across all 15 questions.
03

Scoring

The quiz has a maximum score of 30 marks.

  • Easy: 1 point for each correct answer.
  • Medium: 2 points for each correct answer.
  • Hard: 3 points for each correct answer.
04

Completion

You can click the Submit Quiz button after finishing the quiz.

  • Your answers will be submitted when you click Submit Quiz.
  • If the timer reaches zero, your answers will be submitted automatically.
  • Your score and results will be displayed after submission.

Do Your Best!

Focus, think smart, and give your best shot.
All the best for your quiz!
