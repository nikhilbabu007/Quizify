Statistics Overview

The Statistics module provides a centralized view of your Quizify application's overall performance. From here, administrators can review every quiz attempt made by users and analyze category-wise performance to identify trends, participation levels and learning outcomes. These insights help evaluate user engagement, compare the popularity of different quiz categories and monitor the overall effectiveness of the platform.
